## 💡 Quick Tips for Writing
Whenever you remember a project (whether for a big enterprise, a startup, a local business, or a friend), try to capture:
1. **Who was the client/project?** (Industry, size, or anonymized if NDA applies)
2. **What was their headache/problem before you arrived?** (e.g., lost orders, clumsy Excel sheets, slow manual customer service on WhatsApp, site crashing on peak sales, messy codebase)
3. **What did you build/improve?** (Web app, automation, database, chatbot, cloud architecture)
4. **What was the concrete result/benefit?** (Time saved, orders increased, cost reduced, zero downtime)
5. **Any links, sketches, or screenshots?**
